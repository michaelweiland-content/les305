Why Good People Can Make Bad Ethical Decisions

Behavioral ethics is the branch of psychology that studies human tendencies and external pressures that influence our ethical decision-making. Knowing these helps us name what is shaping the judgment, create a pause, and reach an ethical decision.

Notice the influence: Name what is shaping the judgment, then create a pause, seek independent information, and restore the ethical stakes to the decision.

Pressure-test the decision: Ethical models are tests used to determine whether your decisions are ethical after you have made them and before putting them into action. There are many models, so similar tests are grouped together below.
