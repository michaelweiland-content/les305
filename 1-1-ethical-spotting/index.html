What Issue-Spotting Means

Ethical issue-spotting is the first step in ethical analysis. Before deciding what should happen, recognize and name the ethical problem in the facts.

1. Notice the pressure point.Look for facts involving honesty, loyalty, harm, secrecy, unfairness, rule-breaking, or abuse of power.
2. Name the issue.Match the facts to the category that best explains why the situation is ethically troubling.
3. Separate issue from solution.Issue-spotting does not yet decide what to do. It tells you what kind of ethical problem needs analysis.
